FAQ

Before you book

Details are confirmed with Mohamed directly, so you always get current information.

Ask on WhatsApp
Where do the trips start?

Both trips are planned from Marrakech with a 09:00 departure. Hotel or riad pickup on the Ourika Valley trip is subject to confirmation.

How hard is the walking?

The Tizi N’Oucheg trip includes a mountain walk, and trail conditions near Setti Fatma can change. Tell Mohamed about your group and he can talk through what suits you, including adapting the itinerary.

What should I wear?

Comfortable footwear for mountain paths and clothing suited to the weather on the day. Ask Mohamed for advice closer to your date.

Are lunch and workshops included?

Lunch and any workshops are arranged with local hosts and depend on availability. What is included, and any costs, is confirmed with Mohamed before you book.

How do I book?

Send a WhatsApp message with your preferred date and group size. Mohamed will confirm the details with you. There is no online payment on this site.
